# Human Queue

Items only a person can do or decide (PROCESS.md P8). Judgement calls are never queued: they are
recorded as decisions in DECISIONS.md (PROCESS.md R8; DECISIONS.md "Process — Judgement calls are
decisions, not queue items"). Never a request to close a review thread. Resolved items are struck
through with the resolution and a link.

An open item is a line that is not struck through, written `- [P8 <kind>] [<id>] <text>` (kinds
and ids: PROCESS.md P8); `grep -n '^ *- \[P8' docs/HUMAN_QUEUE.md` lists them. New items are
appended at the end of the file, under "New items".

The lines before "New items" are the queue up to 2026-11-20, kept as history in their original
order: the file frozen on 2026-11-18 first, then the per-unit files of `docs/queue/` (D41) by file
name (DECISIONS.md "Process — One decision log and a P8-only queue (D41 superseded)"). Every line
not yet struck through then (open, or checked `[x]`) is struck through with its disposition:
`Decided:` names the DECISIONS.md section that settles it, `Resolved:` or `Superseded:` gives the
evidence (`path:line` means that line at commit 995935f), `Moved to #<n> (KP-NNN)` names the
GitHub issue that now carries the work, and "Kept open as the P8 line below." marks a line
restated as an open P8 item right after it. Lines that were already struck through are unchanged.
