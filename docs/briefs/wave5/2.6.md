GOAL
  A curved flipper bat shaped as a circular-arc tube that plugs into the existing flipper solver and
  behaves like the straight bat in the limit of large radius.

DELIVERABLES (PLAN 2.6)
  1. `CurvedBanana` as a circular-arc tube implementing `BatShape`: analytic closest point
     (point-to-arc in the bat plane, then tube radius) and surface velocity v_bat = ω_bat × r_contact.
  2. Rotating-arc vs. sphere CCD by conservative advancement in the flipper's local frame. parry3d's
     nonlinear cast is not relied on (it can report the shape pair as unsupported).
  3. Correct handling of the concave and convex faces and of the tip cap, using the same two-body
     impulse as 2.3.

VERIFICATION CRITERIA (PLAN 2.6)
  1. Contacts at 25 %, 50 % and 85 % along the arc give monotonically changing release angles, each
     shifted in a consistent direction against a straight capsule of equal length.
  2. As arc_radius_mm grows large, results converge to the StraightCapsule solver within tolerance
     (state the tolerance and show the convergence rate).
  3. A ball resting in the concave pocket of a raised bat stays cradled for 2,000 ticks.
  4. The 2.3 timing sweep passes on the curved bat with no discontinuity — under the current ruling:
     a dead spot of ≤ 50 % of the held rebound and continuity with every jump explained (the 25 % depth
     is Gate A1's).
  Plus: proptests for closest-point correctness against brute-force sampling, no tunnelling at maximum
  angular velocity over a sweep, energy bound as in 2.3, allocation guard, 10-run determinism.
