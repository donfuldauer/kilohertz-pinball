GOAL
  Chrome balls reflect their surroundings through octahedral probes updated under a strict per-frame
  budget, and the HDR frame from 5.1 goes through a physically based post chain to the display.

DELIVERABLES (PLAN 5.2)
  1. Octahedral reflection probes behind a `ReflectionSource` trait (5.4 will add a ray-query
     implementation of the same trait). Budget: at most 2 probe updates per frame at 128², round-robin
     across balls; balls without a fresh probe reuse the nearest.
  2. HDR chain: physically based bloom, ACES tone mapping, velocity-based motion blur on balls,
     MSAA or SMAA.

5.1 FOLLOW-UPS ASSIGNED TO 5.2 (5.1 review → 5.2 brief)
  * N2: grazing-angle albedo exceeds 1 for dielectrics (pearl 1.35, wood 1.14 at n·v = 0.03; white
    dielectric at roughness 0.1 reaches 1.72) because diffuse is scaled by (1 − F(v·h)) and the coat
    uses Kelemen visibility; bloom will amplify rims — consider (1 − F(n·v)) (a pbr/ shader change;
    update 5.1 goldens only after analytic checks pass, and log it).
  * N4: 5.1 spec snapshots `light_insert_lit` and `ceramic_pearl` clip in 8-bit; add an exposure scale
    before encoding (the tone-mapping stage owns exposure).
  * N7: the chrome highlight peaks near 3000 HDR (roughness 0.06, no AA): add a firefly clamp or a
    bloom threshold.
  * Acrylic draws back faces unculled in index order within one draw; 5.2/5.4 should use two passes
    or refraction — decide, log.

VERIFICATION CRITERIA (PLAN 5.2; `gpu-tests`, lavapipe)
  1. A chrome ball beside a lit red insert shows red in the expected reflection region (analytic:
     region mean colour, red channel ratio).
  2. Bloom energy matches the golden image (and analytically: bloom conserves energy within a stated
     tolerance — total luminance before vs after, minus the threshold part).
  3. With 6 balls, the per-frame probe update count never exceeds the budget (count over ≥ 60 frames;
     every ball's probe age bounded by ⌈6/2⌉ frames).
  Plus: ACES curve matches the reference fit at sample points; motion blur is zero for a still ball and
  aligned with velocity for a moving one; anti-aliasing reduces edge aliasing measurably.

VISUAL CHECKLIST
  Chrome ball shows a recognisable reflected environment and the nearby red insert; bloom halos are soft,
  centred and not blocky; no fireflies; tone-mapped image has no clipped flat regions on the test scenes;
  a fast ball's blur streak points along its velocity.
