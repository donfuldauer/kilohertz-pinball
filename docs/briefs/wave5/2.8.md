GOAL
  Any ball from 15.88 mm to 79.4 mm simulates correctly, and a bundle that declares a ball profile its
  geometry cannot carry is rejected at validation with an error naming the offending feature.

DELIVERABLES (PLAN 2.8)
  1. `jumbo_xl_resin` profile (79.4 mm) and support for any diameter between 15.88 mm and 79.4 mm.
  2. Clearance validator: for every ball profile a table declares, check lane widths, flipper gaps,
     wireform gauge, scoop and VUK diameters, and headroom under upper decks. Violations are bundle-
     validation errors naming the offending feature (file + key path, like pin_assets ValidationError).
     A profile can be restricted to listed layers.
  3. CCD and broad-phase margins that scale with radius.

VERIFICATION CRITERIA (PLAN 2.8)
  1. A 79.4 mm ball against a 15.88 mm ball conserves momentum to the 2.2 tolerances.
  2. The validator rejects an unrestricted XL ball on a table with a 65 mm deck gap and accepts it once
     restricted to the main deck's open area.
  3. No tunnelling for the XL ball against a flipper at maximum angular velocity.
  Plus: proptest sweeps over diameters 15.88–79.4 mm (no tunnelling through thin walls at max speed,
  resting contact stable), validator never panics on generated tables.
