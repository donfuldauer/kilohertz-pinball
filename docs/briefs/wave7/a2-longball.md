GOAL
The soak monitor currently raises EndlessBall for any ball older than endless_ball_ticks (300 000 ticks). At Gate A2 that flagged 3 Apex balls (1 in 3,000 standard games, 2 in 3,000 expert) which were whole-playfield play, drained naturally 7-50 s after the flag, and are the tail of an ordinary ball-time distribution (constant drain rate about 0.021-0.026 /s gives about 1.8 / 4.7 expected >300 s balls per 3,000). Make the monitor distinguish that case: a ball past the limit that later drains naturally after a broad 60 s switch window is reported as LongBall (a reported, non-failing finding). Every other case stays EndlessBall exactly as today. Gate A1 and Gate A2 must stay as strict against traps as they are now.

REQUIREMENTS
1. Add FindingKind::LongBall (rustdoc: ball past the endless limit that later drained naturally after broad play; reported, not failing). fails_soak() is false for it. It gets a JSON name ("LongBall"), a Markdown line/section like the other kinds, and a findings_by_kind entry; FindingKind::ALL grows to 11 (keep report order: put it right after EndlessBall) and every exhaustive match/const array sized by ALL is updated. TickBudget keeps timing-only handling.
2. Monitor rule (fixed-size state per ball slot sized at GameMonitor::new; no per-tick allocation; the sim must not change): when a ball's age passes endless_ball_ticks, do not emit yet. Record a pending state for that slot: the finding as it would be emitted today (same tick, subject, detail text, including "longer than the <N> ms limit"), plus the broad-window verdict evaluated over the 60 s switch window at that tick (the existing 60 one-second bucket counters). Broad means: at least 8 distinct non-cabinet switches credited in the window AND the two most-hit switches together are under 50 % of the window's hits. (Make the two numbers named constants with rustdoc, e.g. LONG_BALL_MIN_SWITCHES = 8, LONG_BALL_TOP2_MAX_PERCENT = 50, compare with integers.) Also evaluate the window again at the moment of resolution is NOT required; the window at the limit is the rule.
3. Resolution:
   a. The ball later drains naturally (its handle is gone from the table via the vanished-ball path in observe_balls AND balls_escaped did not rise for it, i.e. it was not an EscapedBall) AND the window verdict was broad => emit one LongBall (the pending finding with kind LongBall, its original tick, and detail text unchanged plus a suffix such as "; drained at tick T after S s"). No EndlessBall for that ball.
   b. Drained naturally but the window was narrow => emit the pending finding as EndlessBall with its original tick and unchanged detail text.
   c. Still on the table when the game ends for any reason (end_at_cap, game over with the ball present, finish), or escaped, or non-finite, or reported StuckBall (existing rule: a ball already reported stuck gets no EndlessBall) => emit as EndlessBall today's way. end_at_cap must still produce EndlessBall for every ball still on the table, also for balls that were never past the limit (unchanged).
   d. Emission order: findings are appended in the order they are resolved; a pending finding keeps its ORIGINAL tick field. Make sure the report's finding list and per-game finding handling stay deterministic (same seed => byte-identical report).
   e. A game must never end with an unresolved pending state: finish() flushes every pending slot as EndlessBall.
